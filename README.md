# Nina — IVF Journal

A premium, private IVF journaling companion designed to feel emotionally safe and commercially credible from day one.

## Product direction

Nina is built around two jobs:

1. Clinical clarity: capture treatment phase, cycle day, energy, body notes, symptoms, appointment notes, and exportable history.
2. Emotional softness: make daily journaling feel beautiful, private, feminine, and safe enough for hard days.

## Current MVP

- Premium landing-style app shell with sticky navigation
- Mobile-first IVF journal experience
- Day-by-day entries saved locally in the browser
- IVF phase tracking and visual journey meter
- Emotional weather selector
- Energy tracking
- Body notes, heart notes, gratitude, and private letter fields
- Gentle daily prompts and affirmations
- Recent-entry timeline
- JSON export for backup / future migration
- Local-first privacy promise
